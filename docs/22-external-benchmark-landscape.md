# 22 — Benchmark landscape and differentiation

Relevant categories: SWE-bench style bug-fix tasks, multi-step code-evolution benchmarks, agent harness optimizers, static architecture linters and maintainability studies. They motivate a better evaluation but do not prove our method novel.

Our distinguishing **research hypothesis**: user-confirmed future intentions and Meta-generated challenge scenarios can help choose boundaries that lower independently measured cost of a held-out feature change, **holding verification constant**. This remains untested.

Prior-art due diligence checklist: compare scenarios and metrics to published long-horizon code benchmarks; identify overlap with harness optimization, scenario-based architecture analysis, robust regret decisions and agent evaluation; add dates/links as verified. Never claim first-ever architecture foresight engine, unique Monte Carlo, or research novelty without careful search.
