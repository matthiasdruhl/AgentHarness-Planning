# ForeCode — judge-facing evidence and demo success gates
**Prepared October 27, 2026.** This is a planning **readiness checklist**, not a forecast of scores or proof features are implemented. Rubric ambiguity is tracked in [rules verification](RULES-VERIFICATION-2026-10-27.md): public secondary reports of waiver give **30% impact / 25% Meta / 20% technical / 25% demo**, whereas prior participant-note summary in this repo said four equal quarters. **Accepted organizer terms prevail; exact weights require owner confirmation.** Build to all four regardless.

## Four judge questions and evidence the judge must actually see

| Dimension | Judge's question | Visible proof (not backstage claims) | FAIL / fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Impact** | Why would a real engineering team care? | Booking team's future permissions demand -> credible tradeoff decision; what a wrong abstraction would cost in regressions/edits/actual known expense; explain exact audience, scope, that one example is a case study | If measured saving absent, discuss an honest decision audit trail, no invented productivity percentage |
| **Meaningful Meta use** | What does Meta do that a template or ordinary autocomplete cannot? | **Actual eligible Meta response** forms a testable architectural prediction **and** a counterexample challenging itself, with exact provider/version/origin and approved intent; conditional post-evidence interpretation | If no real eligible Meta inference, **qualifying-model claim blocked**. Label mock/recorded/template honestly; Muse Code writing the product is not enough |
| **Technical depth & execution** | Is this genuinely hard, safe and working? | Show actual matched coding-agent edits if approved, protected checks bound to exact post-agent tree, tamper/forgery rejected, safety controls, provenance and a loss/failed case | If agents not approved: demonstrate real no-inference grader, but **no agent comparison claim**; avoid private-oracle exposure |
| **Demo and credibility** | Can I understand and trust the product in under three minutes? | v4.4 editorial landing -> Atlas/Time Machine (actual source-backed topology) -> Meta Scientist -> recorded real test result with check ledger -> scoped certificate; keyboard/clean browser/replay fallback | Never represent recorded as live, mock as measured or synthetic as protected; maintain clear absent-evidence flow |

## Five judge-visible acceptance decisions

- **J1 — New-work provenance:** first **0:00–0:12** of the video separates pre-hackathon concept/planning from all features built and demonstrated after **Oct 26 noon ET**; public repo has new, independently auditable implementation, no planning code/assets imported. All contributors organizer-approved; copyright/IP and track resolved.
- **J2 — Meta uniqueness:** judge can point to *one actual eligible model-generated falsifiable prediction* and *one distinct proposed counterexample*. Each binds to confirmed future intention and specific candidate IDs; where evidence exists the model interprets results without deciding verification. Invalid/denied prompt must fail closed.
- **J3 — Experimental validity:** judge can inspect two architectures and the **same frozen future requirement**; any claim B beats A requires initially-correct architectures, same agent/tools/runtime/oracle/limits, real post-agent results and B preparation cost. No case-specific Meta incremental claim without credible equally informed non-Meta C.
- **J4 — Evidence accountability:** at least one actual authenticated private-grader acceptance result is shown with scope and check IDs, and one actual denied/rejected/insufficient result if available. Strictly distinguish offline fixed-scenario no-inference acceptance from **real coding-agent** runs; cannot claim the former proves the latter. Unknowns explicit.
- **J5 — Presentation readiness:** hosted read-only app and 3-minute video accessible in clean judge browser, accessible keyboard/mobile, no credentials, correct URLs and no shell execution endpoint, tested production build and exact recorded result path. Owner confirms portal submission before **Nov 4 9 PM ET**.

## Impact-first communication overlay (Oct 29)

The existing booking/team-permissions experiment is the concrete product story; do not switch to e-commerce. Show the **working booking action today**, the **specific future delegated-permissions need**, the **possible security/rework consequence as a hypothesis**, and, when measured, the actual independent outcome. Close with an **evidence-backed refactor / keep / inconclusive Decision Report**, not solely a verification certificate. A safe user-facing permission example is optional only if tied to the actual recorded evaluated tree/check; otherwise a truthful diagram is sufficient. Negative and tied outcomes are valuable rather than embarrassing. See [detailed impact specification](IMPACT-AND-DEMONSTRABILITY-REFINEMENT-2026-10-29.md) for exact content, gates, honest fallback and acceptance checks.

**Video first, features second:** by **Oct 30** capture a low-fidelity complete walkthrough; by **Nov 1** cut to 3 minutes and validate claims against visible source/receipts; **Nov 2 feature freeze**; Nov 3 finalize. A beautiful UI without actual proof loses credibility.

## Acceptance rehearsal matrix

- [ ] A judge unfamiliar with the project can say **what it does** in one sentence after 20 seconds.
- [ ] The judge can distinguish **Meta's prediction** from **independent Verify's verdict** at a glance.
- [ ] In 10 seconds, the judge can see **what actually changed in source** and why.
- [ ] Demo demonstrates **one real failure/negative or explicit insufficient state**, not just celebratory green.
- [ ] Every displayed number either has a trusted originating result, is labeled projected, or is unknown.
- [ ] Comparison includes costs of preparation and no task/evaluator inequality.
- [ ] Scope of success is limited to one pinned booking case; no broad superiority claims.
- [ ] Public access via private/incognito session; read-only API and safe model consent boundaries.
- [ ] Video source/new-work disclosure, one correct theme/track, all approved contributors, valid license.
- [ ] Record exact local playback artifact ID, checked public links, video duration and portal receipt.

**Gate definition:** All J1–J5 must be *truthfully* adjudicated (pass, blocked with explanation, or owner decision) by Nov 2, not simply checked off by document existence. Distinguish *minimum honest submission* from *credible top-prize contender*: latter needs live Meta reasoning **and** fair measured downstream benefit/insight, reliably depicted.
