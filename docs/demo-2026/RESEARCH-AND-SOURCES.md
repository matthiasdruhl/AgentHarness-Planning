# ForeCode demo research and source register

Research date: November 1, 2026, America/New_York. **Planning only. No media was captured, produced or uploaded; no inference or experiment was run.** Start with [compliance](META-RULES-COMPLIANCE.md), then [the capability audit](EVIDENCE-CLAIM-MATRIX.md), [storyboard](THREE-MINUTE-STORYBOARD.md), [narration](NARRATION-DRAFT.md), [shots](SHOT-LIST.md), [contingencies](CONTINGENCY-VERSIONS.md) and [judge critique](JUDGE-CRITIQUE.md).

## Research conclusion

Make one decision understandable: **the booking app works; team permissions are coming; should its developer preserve today's design or prepare a policy boundary?** Let judges see a booking succeed, inspect Meta's prediction and challenge, then see exactly why the evidence supports a decision or requires abstention. Start product action at 0:12, immediately after the required provenance disclosure. The current defensible version is **Path C**, with genuine recorded Meta reasoning and an inconclusive Decision Report. A documented private past run is not an approved public result.

There is no established formula for winning, and these examples do not show that a particular edit or pacing choice caused an award. The second-level timings below are ForeCode-specific editorial recommendations, not measured attention thresholds or Meta rules.
