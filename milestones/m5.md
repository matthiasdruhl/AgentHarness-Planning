### Contract Freeze v1 deterministic scoring gate
Use normative `ScoreBasis` and the fixed formula in [Contract Freeze v1](../docs/CONTRACT-FREEZE-v1.md): upfront action weights (3 for introduce_boundary; 2 for extract/move/add_interface; 0 for leave_unchanged); downstream = 2 × projected changed modules + projected route changes + 2 × relevant unresolved dependencies; regret = total - scenario minimum. Complete candidate×scenario matrix, same baseline/units, unique paths/references, minimax recommendation, tie lower upfront then lexically lowest ID; incomparable inputs abstain (`recommendationId:null`). Any old phrase "ordinal scoring" must not authorize arithmetic on arbitrary labels.
