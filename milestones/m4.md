### Contract Freeze v1 consent and revision gate
`IntentDraftV1` is never automatically confirmed; `IntentV1.status=confirmed` requires explicit developer action and `source.kind=user_confirmed`. Revoking/editing increments revision, invalidates active downstream recommendations, retains immutable historical experiment logs. `ProposeRequest` requires explicit `consent:true` and only confirmed intents; `IntentV1.updatedAt` required UTC. See [frozen semantics](../docs/CONTRACT-FREEZE-v1.md).
