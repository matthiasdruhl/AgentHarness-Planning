WHY FORECODE EXISTS

Don't predict
the future.
Interrogate it.

Code assistants make the next change. ForeCode investigates which design might make the changes after that less painful—without treating a model's confidence as proof.

01 /

02 /
Challenge your own assumption.Meta generates a plausible opposing scenario. It stays a visible proposal, never a secretly independent test.
03 /
Measure, don't narrate.Compare the status quo against a purposeful change with equal downstream tasks, tools, budgets, and evaluator policy.
04 /
Keep proof separate from prediction.Independent verification determines whether exact checks passed. Meta can interpret verified evidence, never certify its own answer.

WHAT DOES A CERTIFICATE ACTUALLY PROVE?

A scoped VERIFIED verdict means only that the evaluator's required checks passed against the exact evaluated code tree under a frozen, authenticated policy. It does not establish the architectural proposal was optimal, that Meta was responsible for improvement, or that unseen futures will succeed. Failed, stale, missing, or unauthenticated artifacts cannot produce trusted VERIFIED status.
